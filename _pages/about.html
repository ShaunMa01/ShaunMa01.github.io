About Me

I am currently a Postdoc at the School of Software, Shandong University, supervised by Prof. Lizhen Cui. Previously, I received my doctoral degree from the School of Software, Shandong University, under the supervision of Prof. Caiming Zhang.

Contact: xiangma@sdu.edu.cn

🚀 Research Interests

My research interests mainly include time series analysis and multimodal information fusion.

The central aim of my research is to capture the dynamics of our ever-changing world through rigorous scientific inquiry and to bridge the gap between fundamental theory and practical implementation.
